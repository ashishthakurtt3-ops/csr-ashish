In terms of scale, it is the fourth largest company globally to have this validation. Growth to us is about responsibility, resilience, and a firm commitment to sustainability. Sustainability to us isn’t just about the environment; it’s equally about people. We nurture our employees, ensure safety in every operation, empower our customers with end-to-end solutions, and uplift the communities we call home. The Company’s sustainability initiatives cover a wide spectrum. From energy conservation to deploying renewable energy and green energy, using wastes in cement production as alternate fuels and to water conservation, emissions reduction to creating institutionalised mechanisms to monitor environmental risks and strict adherence to the company’s ‘zero non-compliance’ regime and much more, ACC Limited's sustained efforts have helped ingrain the sustainability agenda in the company’s DNA.
