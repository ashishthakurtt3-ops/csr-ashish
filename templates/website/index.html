Institutional Guide & Compliance Reference

Statutory Section 135 Framework & Verified Access
1. Section 135 Regulatory Framework

Under Section 135 of the Companies Act 2013, Indian corporations meeting net worth or net profit thresholds are statutorily mandated to allocate at least 2% of average net profits to approved CSR activities, catalogued through audited BRSR and annual MCA disclosures.

2. Mandatory NGO Accreditation Checklist

To legally partner with listed corporate foundations for statutory CSR grants, non-profit institutions must maintain:

  • MCA Form CSR-1: Unique Registration Number issued by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs
  • Sections 12A & 80G: Valid registration under the Income Tax Act, 1961
  • NITI Aayog NGO-Darpan ID: Verification code registered on the Government of India portal
  • Financial Audits: 3 consecutive fiscal years of audited balance sheets and ITR filings
3. Data Accuracy & Direct Application Policy

Every corporate profile, grant RFP, and job opening on ImpactBridge originates strictly from verified corporate domains and statutory MCA filings. Zero simulated records or generic guessed emails are displayed.
