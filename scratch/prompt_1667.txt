### External website rules

- Official company-owned pages.
- Official linked ATS pages.
- Official external documents.
- Unrelated job aggregators.
- Search results and third-party commentary.
- Unknown or suspicious pages.

Do not use an unknown external URL merely because AI predicts it is relevant.

### Bounded crawling

Add configurable limits for:

- Maximum pages per company.
- Maximum crawl depth.
- Maximum pages per domain.
- Maximum ATS pagination.
- Request timeouts.
- Retries and backoff.
- Per-domain request rate.
- Maximum document size.
- Total run time or request budget.

Use reasonable conservative defaults for the first batch. Reuse existing politeness controls where they already work.

2. A company's broad CSR policy or commitment.
3. A sustainability target.

5. An annual report or CSR disclosure.
6. An active request for proposals or funding opportunity.
7. An unrelated community or marketing activity.

Extract fields only where they are supported:

- Company ID.
- Project name.
- Project description.
- CSR sector or category.
- Geographic location and state.
- Financial year.
- Reported budget, amount sanctioned or expenditure, with its exact meaning.
- Implementation partner, NGO or agency.

- Beneficiary information, where documented.

For procurement opportunities, verify that an actual tender, RFP, grant or application opportunity exists. A historical expenditure announcement does not constitute an open opportunity.

If the report establishes that a company conducted a project but does not establish whether it remains ongoing, preserve the documented historical information without falsely marking it as currently active.

## 11. Apply deterministic verification independently of AI

Develop or strengthen explicit validation rules for:

### Company attribution
- The record relates to the correct company.
- The source belongs to the correct legal entity, subsidiary or verified official ATS.
- Similar company names and ticker mismatches are detected.

### URL integrity
- HTTP and HTTPS URLs are syntactically valid.
- Redirect chains are recorded.
- Unapproved external destinations are rejected or reviewed.
- Localhost, private IP addresses and unsafe redirects are blocked to prevent server-side request forgery.
- URL normalization is used for comparison and deduplication without destroying meaningful paths or query parameters.

### Content integrity
- Required fields contain meaningful source-supported values.
- Navigation labels are not misidentified as records.
- Dates and amounts are consistent with source text.
- Historical and current information are clearly distinguished.
- Duplicate and near-duplicate records are identified.

### Publication safety
- A page classification score alone cannot approve a record.
- A successful HTTP request alone cannot verify its content.
- A valid JSON response from an LLM cannot establish factual correctness.
- Missing data cannot be guessed.
- Conflicting evidence requires review.
- An access failure does not automatically mean a vacancy is expired.

Produce explicit, auditable pass/fail/unknown results for these checks.
