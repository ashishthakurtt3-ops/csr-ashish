Matching Gifts

Employers match the donations their employees make to nonprofits — often 1:1, and sometimes 2:1 or 3:1 — instantly doubling or tripling a gift once the donor submits a simple request.

Volunteer Grants

When employees volunteer their time, many companies donate a fixed amount per hour served — turning volunteered hours into unrestricted cash grants for the causes your donors support.
